# Testing the Flow Studio by hand

The walks a person drives through the studio, besides the tests [`README.md`](README.md#development) runs. Start the studio as the README says.

Each walk below says what each step should show, so a difference is a finding. They were driven in a browser against both backends on 2026-10-09, with no page error; walks 1, 5 and 6 again against the core on 2026-10-21, after the studio's restyle, which changed how a step looks and not what it says. Start each from a fresh page, or *Reset* under *Simulation*, which empties the sandbox. Where the backends word a step differently, both are given.

1. **An example.** Choose `service.yaml`, then the example *A job is not given to an engineer who left* (its name, `a_job_is_not_given_to_an_engineer_who_left`, is the entry's tooltip), and *Run to the end*, or *Step* through it. Each setup step is marked ✓ APPLIED and reads *Applied.*; the request under test is marked ✕ REFUSED and reads *engineer_active does not hold: supply or correct an input and try again.*; the panel ends in green, *Does what the example claims.* *A job opens for an active engineer* ends the same way with its request APPLIED. The page opens on `ServiceJob`, the module's type with the most transitions; the type tabs (`InspectionCheck`, `ServiceJob`) switch the diagram, the rule set and the metrics.
2. **Free play.** *Reset*; under *Create* choose `User`, give a login and a name, and *Request*: *This session* reads *User.add Applied.* Choose `ServiceJob`, pick the user as its engineer, and *Request*. Choose the job under *Objects*: *What servicejob-2 offers* marks each transition available, available with input, or unavailable, and `finish` reads *Not available in OPEN; it is taken from another state.* *Take* `start`, which applies at once, as it takes no input; then *Take* `finish` and *Request* with the form left empty: refused, *The input signed_off_by is not valid: supply or correct an input and try again.* on the core, *An input is not valid: …* on the runner, which does not work out which input and says so on the wire. On the `ServiceJob` tab the metrics read `null` where a metric has no value yet, such as an average over no time worked. *acting as* chooses who sends each request, and *Advance* moves the sandbox's clock by the duration given: the `now` beside it and the *Simulation* panel's both read the new time, `2 h` taking `2026-01-01T00:00:00Z` to `2026-01-01T02:00:00Z`, and a request sent after it is stamped so in its object's history.
3. **A proposal.** Choose `delivery.yaml`, then the example *An approved delivery is handed over*, and *Step* six times, which applies its setup and leaves its request under test next. Choose the delivery under *Objects*: `complete` offers *Take* and *Propose*. *Propose* files it at once, as it takes no input: `proposal-5 Proposal PENDING` under *Objects*. Choose the proposal, which offers `approve`, `reject` and `withdraw`, and *Take* `approve`: *approve on proposal-5 Applied.*, the delivery DELIVERED and the proposal EXECUTED. A proposal filed before the delivery's checklist is ticked, after *Opening creates the checklist*, is refused on approval: *checklist_complete does not hold: another object must change first.*
4. **An import.** Choose `servicedesk.yaml`; under *Import* choose `Employee`, the one mirror type, which *Create* does not list; give a legacy key, `E-17`, and its `employee_id`, and *Import*: *import Employee as E-17 imported*, and `E-17 Employee EMPLOYED` under *Objects*.
5. **A publish.** With `service.yaml` loaded, a user created and a job opened for them (walk 2), choose `service-v2.yaml (design)` under *Publish a version* and *Dry run*. The report reads *service-v2.yaml as version 2: it may be approved*, then five notices, `check:rule_changed`, `check:migration`, `check:warn`, `check:agent_open` and `mapping:backfill:ServiceJob.site`, the last reaching one object, the job; then each invariant the version could break, `ServiceJob.photo_when_done · local` among them, *could be broken by ServiceJob.finish, ServiceJob.open, ServiceJob.start*, and the metric dimensions naming a person. *Publish* installs it: *Installed; migrated, each with its migration in its history*, listing the job, whose history, on its choice, ends in a `migrate` row from OPEN to OPEN. *Withdraw* instead leaves the sandbox as it was.
6. **An edit.** Choose `service.yaml` and *Edit*: *the design's module, read-only · the checker finds nothing wrong*. *Copy to the workspace to edit*: `service.yaml` appears under *Your workspace*; choose it there and *Edit*. Change a `from: OPEN` to `from: NOPE`: after a pause the status reads *1 finding*, the line is marked once scrolled into view, and *Load into the sandbox* is disabled. Put it right, then *Save*, *Saved service.yaml to the workspace.*, and *Load into the sandbox*, *Loaded into a fresh sandbox.*
7. **A session saved as an example.** With the workspace's module loaded (walk 6), create a user in free play; under *Save the session as an example* describe it, *Draft it*, which shows the claim drafted from what the last request did, and *It should: save it*: *Saved to service.examples.yaml: the flow checker ran it, and it holds.*

Walks 6 and 7 write `service.yaml` and `service.examples.yaml` into the workspace, [`flows/`](flows/), which git does not track until they are added; delete them afterwards unless they are to be kept.
