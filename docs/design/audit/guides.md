# Audit area 6: the guides and the gateway contract (promise G)

Checkout 698034b, read-only at `audit/repo`; binary `audit/bin/objectflow-service` (`objectflow-service 0.2.0, serving stores of objectflow-core 0.2.0, commit 698034b`). Rubric: ADR-0204 §§1-5. Everything I ran is under `audit/a6/` (written below as `a6/`).

## 1. How the area was enumerated, and why it is complete

**The sources.** Every page a team reads before it runs or builds on the engine: `README.md`, `SECURITY.md`, `llms.txt`, `docs/guides/README.md`, `quickstart.md`, `concepts.md`, `write-a-flow.md`, `flow-format-card.md`, `integrate.md`, `agents.md`, `operate.md`, `wire.md`, `errors.md`, `wire-v1.json`; the shipped samples (`docs/design/flow-format/examples/*.yaml` with their examples, and `returns-module.md`, which `README.md:97` names as the home of its example); `core/README.md`, because two printed messages send the operator there (`main.rs:3882`, `main.rs:5304`). And every instruction the service prints: the usage text (`main.rs:3845-3882`), every start note and start refusal (`main.rs:4966-5134`, `5163-5352`), every runtime log line that tells someone what to do (`main.rs:4239`, `4318`, `5468`), and every fault or `/health` text that carries advice (`main.rs:1063`, `3264`, `4187`). I found these by grepping `say!(` (29 sites) and the `Err(format!(...))`/`std::io::Error::other` strings in `main.rs` outside its test module (`main.rs:5477` on).

**The instances.** From each page I took every fenced `sh` or `json` block that is a step, every imperative sentence (send, set, refuse, never, run, keep), and every statement about engine behaviour that a gateway or an operator acts on. The gateway contract (ADR-0204 §2) is two lists: `agents.md:189-216` ("Keep it in bounds: in your gateway", reads, retiring) and `integrate.md:5-176`. I checked each instruction on those lists against the engine. In the other direction, I looked up in the guides every engine behaviour a gateway met while I was probing. A sentence that only restates design without a behaviour to check (for example `README.md:9-36`, the case for the product) is out.

**How each instance was run, as production.** I made each store with `--create` in a directory of my own and published its flow through `DeclarationChange` draft, submit and approve, as `operate.md:41-57` says. The procedures ran on:
- `a6/d1`: a Unix socket with the unit's flags;
- `a6/gw`: loopback, with `--evaluator` pointing at a local endpoint I ran (`a6/evaluator.py`, which answers by argument: satisfied, unsatisfied, unanswered, 500, or a 7 s stall) and a bearer token file.

The sandbox tutorials also ran on a sandbox. Tools: `a6/runpage.py` runs a page's `sh` blocks and compares each with the block after it; `a6/publish.py` follows `operate.md`'s publish procedure; `a6/wire.py` is a small client; `a6/runs/gw/gw.yaml` is a test flow with an agent actor type, personal members, an evaluator guard, a proposable transition, a backdating limit and an assignee metric. Three scripts re-run the broken items: `a6/repro-m1.sh`, `a6/repro-m2.sh` and `a6/repro-minors.sh` (output in `a6/runs/repro-minors.out`).

**Why it is complete.** The guides are finite, and each instance below is one block, one instruction or one printed string. The gateway contract is exactly the two lists. The printed instructions are the grep's whole result. No row is a sample of a larger set except the rows marked "class".

**What I left out, and why.**
- **Building.** `cargo build` in `quickstart.md:9-11`, `operate.md:17-23` and `core/README.md`, and `examples-on-core`, which builds with cargo. The brief forbids it.
- **The repository's gates.** `check-corpus.py` (`README.md:158`) and `check-guides.py` are forbidden too. Instead I read what they hold.
- **systemd itself.** I have no root. I emulated `NOTIFY_SOCKET` and `WATCHDOG_USEC`, and I ran each unit's command line by hand.
- **An upgrade from an earlier release.** The only earlier stores are the repository's `.sqlite` fixtures, which I may not open, and I have no older binary.
- **Binding beyond loopback.** I did not do it on a shared host.
- **A leading clock.** It needs the host clock moved.
- **`disk_low`, `disk_full`, `log_blocked`, `writes_failing`.** The suite tests named in the rows hold them.
- **Cost figures** (`operate.md:230-276`) belong to area 4. **P, R, A and V semantics** beyond what a guide states belong to areas 1-3.

## 3. The broken items, most severe first

1. **G-83 / G-114, major (D and G).** When a `-wal` lies beside a restored copy, following the service's printed advice leaves the restore undone, silently.
   - **Smallest fix.** Change the note at main.rs:4979-4985 to say what a restore needs once the start has taken the log in: "where you had just put a backup in the store's place, this start has taken the later log into it: stop the service, remove the -wal and -shm, put the backup in the store's place again, and start". Say the same at operate.md:330. If the fix should not depend on the reader, the service could leave the log out of the stop's checkpoint when the start found foreign frames, but that is area 5's to weigh.
   - **What it would refuse that 0.2.0 accepted.** Nothing.
   - **The check.** A deployment test that follows the note word for word and asserts the backup's state is served.
2. **G-27, major (A, through a guide-shown pattern).** The guides' example identity shape `u-10442` is the shape D669 shadows; reproduced, a person's request was recorded under a service actor.
   - **Smallest fix in this area.** integrate.md:48 and wire.md:33 say an identity must not have the form `<word>-<number>`, and every example identity changes, to `u10442` say.
