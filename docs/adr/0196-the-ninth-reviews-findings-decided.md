# ADR-0196: The ninth review's findings, decided

- **Status:** **Decided** 2026-10-13 on the author's standing instruction, "continue the loop to get the stack ready for production, follow your recommendations", each decision the recommendation stated here
- **Date:** 2026-10-13
- **Refined by:** ADR-0197 — the checks and a dry run's impact linear in a type's transitions and a change's types, a page's memory bounded by reading its events as it goes, a submit of another's draft refused. ADR-0198 — what an expression unfolds to bounded, and a dry run's checks held to its deadline.
- **Refines:** ADR-0195 decisions 1, 4, 6 and 7; ADR-0179 (paging); ADR-0189 decision 2 and ADR-0190 decision 2 (the read pools)
- **Relates to:** `design/production-review.md` §13; `guides/operate.md`; `guides/wire.md`; `guides/agents.md`

## Context

The ninth production review (`production-review.md` §13) found no blocker. Its operator found no major and gave GO; its adversary found four majors, so the bar is not met. N-4: the YAML parser compared each key of a mapping with every earlier one, and a draft parses its source holding the writer, so one draft of a mapping of 100,000 keys held every write 37.8 s. N-10: a `check` of such a draft held a read past every deadline, since a parse touches no store and a read's deadline and stop are checked only where it does, so an erasure's values stayed in the store's files for 38 s and the `-wal` reached 1.9 GB. N-9: a page of `query`, `pull` or `export` had no bound in bytes, 197 MB for 1,000 objects of large strings, 4.78 GB for eight at once. And the eighth review's ERASE-WAL-DRYRUN, fixed for a dry run, stood open through N-10. Measuring them found more of the same kind: a change's checks grew with the square of the types it declares, 8,000 types running past eleven minutes in the dry run's one slot (N-8 met one such path, a literal comparing `state` 128,000 times, 229 s).

## Decision

1. **What a change holds is parsed and checked in time that grows with it, not with its square.** A mapping's keys are found repeated by a set; a type's check reads the module's enumerations, states and supersessions once for every type, never every type's again for each; a text's compared states are found walking it once. A draft's parse, a `check` of it and a dry run's checks then take a moment for anything a request can carry: 100,000 keys parse in 0.1 s, and a module of 8,000 types checks in 3.3 s, where they took 37.8 s and past eleven minutes. A test holds the checks' growth to about linear, and one the parse's. (N-4, N-8, N-10, and the open part of ERASE-WAL-DRYRUN)
2. **A page of `query`, `pull` or `export` ends at 8 MiB as at its limit**, as `history` does: one item at least, a cursor going on from the last it holds, and an `export` page never among the rows of one cursor. `pull` and `export` say `more` where a page ended at its limit or its bytes, since their cursor is never `null` and a page with fewer items than its limit no longer means the log is read to its end. (N-9)
3. **A wait for another process's lock is looked for on a tick of its own**, every second, reading only what the store tells, never the writer, so the log says it within seconds of a write's first retry, where on the log's cut's tick it was said only once the write gave up. (ADR-0195 decision 6, partly fixed)
4. **A query that reads its type whole waits in the analytics pool**, beside the metrics, never in a person's worklist slot: one whose filter neither requires its states nor holds a column equal to a value, ordered or filtered, where ten such filters left a plain worklist query timing out and a person's `available` waiting 17.5 s. (operator 2)
5. **On a change another actor drafted, only an actor that may approve the next version may revise, reopen, refresh, withdraw or reject it**, in both backends, refused `invariant_violated`, `publish_taken_by`, `delegable`: where no `publishing` is declared, an agent may act on its own changes only, where it rejected, reopened and revised a person's. A version whose `publishing` names a kind its installed version's rule, or its default, did not let approve is noticed `taken_by_dropped`, where a version naming `agent` after none raised nothing. (N-1)
6. **A version that drops `personal` from an attribute is noticed**, `personal_dropped`, in both checkers: an erasure no longer reaches the attribute's values, which stay. (N-11)
7. **Requests answer refusals, not `501`**: a request of a change's `publish` transition is refused `not_requestable`, naming `publish` as its operation, in both backends; a metric read's filter a row cannot answer is refused `malformed_filter`, its reason short, never a `501` whose fault carries what it met. (N-2, N-3)
8. **The operator is the identity `operator` in any ASCII case, everywhere**: an approval's exception for the operator and `upgrade`'s check resolve the actor as every request does, where a request and `maintain` took `OPERATOR` and `upgrade` refused it. The guides say the identity is reserved. (N-7)
9. **The minors**: the dry run's slot, waited for past its deadline, names `--dry-run-timeout`, and a late dry run blames a large store or a large change; `/health` answers a store file it cannot read `503 store_unreadable`, where it answered `500 internal`; a start notice names a `-wal` it replays as what a restore must remove, and one where no `--attempt-retention` is given, refused attempts being kept for ever; a token holding a control character is refused at the start; a query's `order` names at most 8 keys and its `fields` at most 256, each once, else `400`; the evaluator's timeout is logged by its flag; a store directory that does not exist, and one the service cannot write, are said so; the guides fix the case-pair migration (its `description`), the `-wal`'s peak (about 350 MiB), `MemoryMax` (the unit's 4G sized for two years, the rule for more), installing the binary, releases not yet published, the unit's hardening, the Unix socket as the access control where loopback TCP lets every local user call, `errors.md`'s `409` and `404`, and the gateway's list: every operation on a `DeclarationChange`, `check` of a draft among them, a label's `forget` on another's label, and the operator's identity in any case.
10. **Unchanged**: pools are chosen by the actor a read names, which the gateway sets (N-6); one erasure every few seconds stops the dry runs it meets, each answered to be sent again (N-5); a backup copied over a running service's file in place is not detected, since SQLite's own checkpoints and an upgrade's second service change the file too, and the guide's restore stops the service first and says what happens otherwise; a refused attempt keeps its inputs until `--attempt-retention` prunes it.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Parsing a change's source before taking the writer** (decision 1): with a parse linear in the source, 2 MiB parses in 0.3 s; moving it out of the request's decision would split one transaction's checks in two for a cost no longer there.
- **A deadline checked inside the checker** (decision 1): it bounds a check found slow but leaves it slow; the checks are made linear, and a test holds them so.
- **Capping a module's types or a mapping's keys** (decision 1): a bound below what a request carries refuses real modules for a cost the linear checks no longer have.
- **Detecting a file copied in place by its modification time** (decision 10): SQLite's automatic checkpoints and a second service during an upgrade write it too, so it would say a store replaced that is not.
- **Refusing a read naming an actor the store does not hold** (decision 10): a read authorizes nothing, and the gateway names the actor; the pools follow what it says.

## Consequences

- `/v1` answers `more` on `pull` and `export`, and pages that end at 8 MiB; a `query`'s `order` and `fields` are bounded; `publish` as a request, an approval-free actor on another's change and a metric filter a row cannot answer are refused where they answered or ran. Before 1.0.0 this costs no promise (ADR-0180).
- `/health` has `store_unreadable`; the checkers have `personal_dropped`, and `taken_by_dropped` covers a widening of `publishing`.
- Each fix is held by a test that fails without it, and the majors are measured again (`defects.md` D621 to D624).
