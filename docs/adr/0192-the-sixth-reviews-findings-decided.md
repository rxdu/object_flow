# ADR-0192: The sixth review's findings, decided

- **Status:** **Decided** 2026-10-13 on the author's standing instruction, "continue the loop to get the stack ready for production, follow your recommendations", each decision the recommendation stated here; built 2026-10-13, in the order below, decision 7's second service said and not refused, as it says.
- **Date:** 2026-10-13
- **Refines:** ADR-0055 and ADR-0171 (the invariants' affected set), ADR-0190 decisions 2, 3 and 4 (the read pools, a filter's cost, an actor's identity), ADR-0179 (pages), ADR-0087 (erasure)
- **Relates to:** `design/production-review.md` §10; `DESIGN.md` §5.6, §9; `guides/wire.md`; `guides/integrate.md` §6, §9

## Context

The sixth production review (`production-review.md` §10) found one blocker and four majors. The blocker, P8, was the fifth review's fix turned against the store: the invariant the notice `unbounded_reach` asks for made every write sweep its type, since the core checked every invariant reading beyond its own object on its whole type after every write, where `DESIGN.md` §5.6 says such an invariant is checked from the related objects. None of the findings needs a choice the PRD leaves to the author but the ones decided here.

## Decision

1. **A traversal invariant is checked from what was written**, as §5.6 says: each chain of relationship ends an invariant reads from its object, every prefix of it, is walked back from each object a request writes, as it was and as it is, through the end that object stores or the indexed column of the end the other side stores, to the objects of the invariant's type; `referrers` is walked back through the written object's own references. An invariant reading what no chain tells, a type scan, a metric or an observation, is still checked on its type whole. Rejected: holding an aggregate over a composite's own set alone, which leaves a path through another type, `category.name`, sweeping.
2. **Each listener counts its own connections** against its own cap, so `--metrics-bind` answers whatever the main listener holds (F9).
3. **An agent's reads wait in a pool of their own**: every read that waits its turn, by an actor of the kind `agent`, takes a slot of an agents' pool of `--max-reads`, never a person's worklist slot or an analytic's; and a query filter calls no metric and holds at most 256 nodes, refused `malformed_filter` past either, where a per-object metric and an `or`-tree of 10,000 comparisons ran to the deadline (P5, the FILTER-COST class). Rejected: fairness by actor, since the engine trusts the application's naming of actors and a buggy client names any; a cost model, which would be a guess.
4. **An erased actor's identity is replaced by its object's id wherever the store keeps it**: an erasure that clears an actor's identity rewrites the events' actor and principal, the attempts' actor, the intervals' `entered_by`, the idempotency records' actor and a proposal's and a change's recorded actors to the actor's object id, which names no person, read by indexes on those columns, which a store opened without them is given; the guides say to choose opaque identities. Rejected: refusing `personal` on an identity, which hides that an email is personal data; recording every event by object id, which changes every event's wire form and every store.
5. **`history` answers pages and waits its turn**: at most `limit` events, 1 to 1,000 and 100 unless asked, with the cursor of the next page, as `query` (ADR-0179), a group's history among it; `history` and `events` wait in the worklists' pool, an agent's in its own (HISTORY).
6. **An identity is what it shows, once**: an identity is not empty, holds no whitespace but inner spaces and no character that shows nothing, a format character, a variation selector, a combining grapheme joiner, a Hangul filler, a tag character or a line or paragraph separator, and is not shaped as an object's id, `<type>-<number>` for a declared type; `identity_taken` compares identities without case; and an import may not admit `identity_taken`. Rejected for now: normalising Unicode forms and confusable letters, which needs tables a dependency carries and the author has not approved; the guides tell the application to give identities in one form.
7. **The minors without a decision of their own are fixed**: a derived time past the calendar answered null in a query's fields too; a read of a store gone from its path `503 store_replaced`; a supersession group walked once by its erasure; an idempotency key compared by the resolved actor; an evaluator's answer taking a key once; a division by a money zero and a nested set `malformed_filter`; an ill-typed filter `malformed_filter`, and no stored value in any fault's reason; an upgrade-due 503's `code`; a store file the service cannot write refused at start; a write refused for a full disk said by `/health` until one commits; a second service on a store said at its start, not refused, since ADR-0178 decision 2 lets an upgrade's old copy share the store; the build's commit in `--version` where the build gives one; and the guides' `time-sync.target`, the stalled write's wait, a metric guard's cost under the writer, the binary's glibc and what to do when an erasure is refused `over_limit`.
8. **Unchanged**: an erasure past a limit stays refused, its remedy the bound the notice names, which decision 1 makes cheap; the one dry-run slot stays one, a change's dry runs withheld from agents at the gateway as `integrate.md` §6 says.

## Consequences

- `/v1` refuses or pages what it took whole: a filter calling a metric or past 256 nodes, `history` past its page, the identities decision 6 names. Before 1.0.0 this costs no promise (ADR-0180).
- A store opened by this release is given the indexes decision 4 reads by; decision 1 reads by the stored ends' indexes every store has (`storage-schema.md` §5); an erased actor's earlier records name its object's id.
