## What the author decided

All thirteen open questions were ruled on 2026-09-08, recorded as ADR-0065 to ADR-0072 and marked Accepted rather than pending; ADR-0073 followed and was confirmed the same day. The table in [`docs/design/declaration-syntax.md`](docs/design/declaration-syntax.md) §11 gives each answer and where it lives; §12 says what remains unestablished.

Five changed the language: a machine's creation guards bind any creation that replaces it, a cascade clause carries arguments, a third tracking mode for types that track no physical thing, a comparable type name on a reverse reference, and both state spellings in both places. Four refused a change: money keeps its currency in the declaration, evaluators stay verdict-only with a mirror for anything that assigns, confidentiality stays per object, and an authority list stays optional at a closed state. One closed a question already answered elsewhere. One kept a rule and changed what is reported. One deferred, and one agreed in principle without building it.

**Two answers moved because of evidence rather than argument.** Per-attribute confidentiality looked likely and turned out to have no need in the first consumer at all. The money counter turned out to rest on a mechanism that consumer does not use, since it stores no stock level and computes availability instead.

## Challenges from the first consumer

Evidence from `~/RduWs/wr_inventory_management` that bore on accepted ADRs. Recorded 2026-09-07; all six resolved in design iteration 1 by ADR-0019 to ADR-0025 (pending author review), with the worked rationale in [`docs/design/first-consumer-walkthrough.md`](docs/design/first-consumer-walkthrough.md) §4. Kept here as the record of what the evidence was.

1. [x] **Atomic outcomes across references, not only parts.** Completing a Delivery moves each reserved unit to `SOLD` and creates its warranty contracts in the same transaction (`wr:app/core/state_registry.py:761-770`, side effects `_update_delivery_items_to_sold`, `_create_delivery_warranties`). Units outlive deliveries, so by ADR-0003's test they are references, not parts. The model describes composite-to-part cascades only (ADR-0004), and ADR-0007, ADR-0012 and ADR-0013 together route cross-object consequences to consumers through at-least-once events. That leaves a window in which a Delivery is `DELIVERED` and its units are still `RESERVED` — an inconsistent state of exactly the kind the project exists to prevent. Options not yet weighed: a transition that declares outcomes on referenced objects, each gated normally and committed atomically; a multi-object transaction primitive; or accepting the window and making it explicit in the declaration. Affects ADR-0004, 0007, 0012, 0013. **Resolved by ADR-0019.**
2. [x] **Automatic transitions on the critical path.** The operations design's auto-fill: when a soft-pegged inbound unit reaches `AVAILABLE` at intake commit, its peg firms into `RESERVED` with no human step, specifically to close the double-booking window (`wr:docs/proposals/operations-system-design.md` §4 "Auto-fill on receipt", §5 step 5). Under ADR-0012 that becomes a consumer reacting to an event, which reopens the window. Whether this is a case of item 1 (an outcome declared on a referenced object) or a genuine trigger is the question. Affects ADR-0012. **Resolved by ADR-0019.**
3. [x] **Derived views are used deliberately.** Slot state (`UNFILLED` / `FILLED` / `FULFILLED`) is computed from foreign keys and the bound unit's status and is "NOT persisted as a column" (`wr:app/models/enums.py`, `SlotState`); the operations design's single-path rules say "demand is derived, not stored" (§9.0 rule 8). ADR-0004 rejects derived *lifecycle* state, and rightly — but slots have no transitions of their own; they are a projection over Delivery items and units. Resolve by wording in ADR-0004: derived state that has transitions is rejected; derived read-only views are the query surface's business. Reopen only if slots turn out to need transitions. Affects ADR-0004. **Resolved by ADR-0021.**
4. [x] **Concurrency is load-bearing and undocumented.** Double-booking was one of three motivating pains in the operations design (§1). The fix was a row lock on reservation and a `version` column on every entity with 409 on a stale write (`wr:docs/DESIGN.md` §2.6); the inventory ADR-0002 notes the TOCTOU hole a service-layer check leaves. ObjectKeeper's DESIGN.md never mentions concurrency control. Tracked as an open model question above. **Resolved by ADR-0023.**
5. [x] **Time-driven transitions from day one.** `WarrantyContract` `ACTIVE → EXPIRED` on "contract expires" (`wr:docs/design/state-transition-system.md`); the alert catalogue lists low stock, procurement overdue, backorder aging, delivery date at risk, lease overdue and warranty expiring, all time-class (`wr:docs/proposals/operations-system-design.md` §6). ADR-0012 places these upstairs. The first consumer therefore needs a scheduler and the "inspectable but unexecuted timing metadata" answer at the same time as the core, not later. Affects ADR-0012. **Resolved by ADR-0022.**
6. [x] **Deletion is a second lifecycle mechanism.** Every entity has `is_deleted` (`wr:docs/DESIGN.md` §7.3); a cascade registry deletes children, and its pre-delete hooks call `force_transition`, which bypasses guards (`wr:docs/design/state-transition-system.md`, "Integration with Cascade System"; `wr:app/core/state_transition.py:577`). The inventory ADR-0003 found that soft-deleting one redundant customer row would have cancelled 102 deliveries, 43 services and 120 warranty contracts. ObjectKeeper's docs never say what deletion is. A terminal transition gated on live references would have refused that cascade with remedy class `dependent`. Tracked as an open model question above. **Resolved by ADR-0024.**

## Data import

The port of the first consumer's production data is a designed path (ADR-0015, DESIGN.md "Data import"). Its properties were derived in discussion and are listed under Confirmations outstanding; the design work is here.

- [x] **Override path.** Decided by ADR-0040 and ADR-0054: a declared asserting transition for repair, gated on a capability with a mandatory reason and recording what it stepped over; a built-in assertion for import and migration, so no type is unimportable by omission.
- [x] **Validation report.** [`docs/design/publish-and-import.md`](docs/design/publish-and-import.md) §2 and §6, 2026-09-09. Three severities, and the per-class disposition is a committed file with four outcomes — undecided, clean upstream, admit, exclude — that an import refuses to run past while any class is undecided. An admitted violation becomes an admission the object carries, which `exceptions(type)` returns until the invariant holds again.
- [x] **Legacy history.** Read-only entries of kind `legacy` carrying their original payload, attached to the object and returned by `history` alongside ObjectKeeper's own events (DESIGN.md §11).
- [x] **Soft-deleted rows.** Land in the type's declared deleted terminal state with provenance `asserted` (ADR-0024). Per-type naming of that state is import mapping work, not design.
- [x] **Legacy keys.** Preserved as `external: legacy` attributes with `lookup` by them (ADR-0018, ADR-0037).
- [x] **Files.** Content-addressed `file` attributes with the bytes in the deployment's blob store ([ADR-0017](docs/adr/0017-file-attachments-are-content-addressed-references.md), confirmed 2026-09-08). Legacy photos, packing-list PDFs and label templates port as controlled attributes written by the import's asserting transition. The blob store's own lifecycle expiry must be disabled.
- [x] **Cutover.** **Staged by object type**, decided by the author 2026-09-09, ADR-0075. Ownership is per type and absolute, so the order is forced: a type may migrate only after every type that references it has, and a cycle in the reference graph moves as one stage. A type held here and owned elsewhere is declared `mirror`.

## Toward implementation

The artefacts planning needs. Two are now done: the declaration syntax and the first consumer's types written in it. Each item is WHAT, not HOW.

- [x] **Concrete declaration syntax.** A file format for types, machines, transitions, guards and outcomes that a person can read and diff, with the version-2 expression language given a grammar. The readable rule set is a rendering of it.
- [x] **Compute the first consumer's stage order.** [`docs/design/first-consumer-cutover.md`](docs/design/first-consumer-cutover.md), 2026-09-09. Seven stages over 45 tables, one cycle, which moves as one stage (D201 withdrew the claim that nullable edges let it span two). Only nine of 42 components are pinned, so the plan has room. **The order cannot be used as it stands**: the stage unit is a type and its tables are not its types, which is the next task.
- [ ] **Map the first consumer's tables to ObjectKeeper types, then recompute the stage order.** Seven photo tables become set-valued attributes, six association tables become derived ends or association types, the audit log becomes the event log and is not a type at all. Roughly twenty types from forty-five tables. Until that mapping exists the stage order is evidence about shape rather than a plan.
- [ ] **Sample three JSON columns against production rows.** `delivery_configurations.packing_checklist` is documented as keyed by delivery-item id; if it is, that table joins the one cycle and makes it a five-member component. This is the single ambiguity with the largest effect on the order, and reading the code cannot settle it.
- [x] **Storage schema.** [`docs/design/storage-schema.md`](docs/design/storage-schema.md), 2026-09-09. Three layers, the type-to-table mapping for both backends, the four indexes a guarantee depends on, and what publishing emits. Its SQLite statements are executed by `scripts/check-schema-doc.py` on every corpus run; the PostgreSQL column and the exclusion constraints are reasoned and unexecuted. Gapless global positions under concurrency is the open item and the first thing to measure.
- [x] **Library API.** [`docs/design/library-api.md`](docs/design/library-api.md), 2026-09-09. Fourteen operations, the verdict as a value rather than an exception, and a Python binding chosen because the first consumer is Python. `scripts/check-api-doc.py` executes it and holds the operation set against DESIGN.md §10, so the two cannot drift. Async, streaming and the exception list are open.
- [x] **Publish and import tooling.** [`docs/design/publish-and-import.md`](docs/design/publish-and-import.md), 2026-09-09. Publishing as one transaction with a three-severity report, mappings as declaration text applied per object as recorded migrations, and import as version zero gated on a committed disposition file that an import refuses to run past while any class is undecided. The extract format and whether a partial import resumes are open.
- [x] **Renderers.** [`docs/design/renderers.md`](docs/design/renderers.md), 2026-09-09. Three projections and one rule: a renderer never restates a guard's logic where it will be read as the rule, because that is the sixth copy of the thing ADR-0010 exists to end. Tool schemas name the operation and point at `availability`. The schema dialect and whether a rule-set diff is textual or semantic are open.
- [x] **Adversarial test harness.** [`docs/design/adversarial-harness.md`](docs/design/adversarial-harness.md), 2026-09-09. Eight falsifiable failures checkable from outside the store, four fallible behaviours rather than a clever attacker, the first consumer's types as the fixture, and a run reduced to its shortest failing prefix. How the racer schedules deterministically is its one hard problem and is open.
- [x] **Prototype the first consumer's unit and delivery types** in the declaration syntax before any runtime exists, to check that the walkthrough survives contact with a concrete format.

## Domain questions to answer from real workflows

These determine model scope. The first consumer's repository answers several; the rest remain open.

- [x] **What proportion of needed guards are local to one object versus queries over related ones?** **Counted 2026-09-09 over the whole of `~/RduWs/wr_inventory_management`: 454 guard sites, of which 238 are one permission predicate repeated. Of the 205 that read data at all, 94 are local to the object and 111 reach beyond it — an even split, not the near-total relational skew a five-predicate sample had suggested. The depth is what sizes the language: 27 one-hop, 31 aggregates over an owned collection, 30 type-wide existence, 23 two-hop of which 21 also aggregate, and **nothing anywhere reaches three hops**. The earlier partial answer was wrong on its own sample too — `_intake_ready` reads only its own columns.*
- [x] **Do type-level invariants occur in practice?** Yes. Two observed: one open engagement per unit, enforced by a partial unique index (inventory ADR-0002); exactly one active configuration version per SKU, enforced across three activation paths (`wr:docs/design/business-processes.md`). ADR-0009 stands; evidence attached to it.
- [x] **Does any axis exist that is genuinely 1:1 with an object for its whole life and has gated transitions of its own?** No. The inventory ADR-0002 found three orthogonal axes on a unit and resolved them as separate objects, not regions. ADR-0003 stands; evidence attached to it.
- [x] **Are there transitions where authority differs from capability?** Yes, and the list is short so far. Two observed: an agent may create a Service but may not be its engineer-of-record (`wr:docs/agent-operations.md` §3); cancelling a placed procurement order or reverting a committed intake needs `PROCUREMENT_CANCEL`, admin-only, because it undoes a committed obligation (`wr:app/models/enums.py`, `Permission`). A short list makes proposals an edge case pending the actor model.
